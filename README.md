# Spirit Shelf

Spirit Shelf is a clean alcohol discovery platform that helps you understand bottles instead of guessing at them.  
You get clear recommendations, simple rankings, and an AI cocktail bot so you pick the right bottle every time.

No jargon.  
No noisy reviews.  
Just confident choices.

## What problem it solves

Buying alcohol is confusing.

Reviews are subjective.  
Tasting notes are vague.  
Most platforms talk like insiders.

Spirit Shelf strips the noise and gives you direct answers so you know what is worth buying for your taste, budget, and skill level.

## What you can do

- Discover spirits using simple filters
- See ranked bottles based on real value
- Use an AI cocktail bot to get drink recommendations
- Get suggestions based on taste, budget, and experience level
- Learn bottles without industry jargon

## AI cocktail bot

The AI cocktail bot helps you decide what to drink or make.

You tell it:
- What flavors you like
- Your budget
- Your skill level

It gives you:
- Cocktail suggestions
- Bottle recommendations
- Simple explanations

No mixology lectures. Just usable answers.

## How Spirit Shelf works

1. Explore spirits using clear filters
2. Check rankings to see what is actually worth buying
3. Ask the AI cocktail bot for recommendations
4. Buy with confidence

Fast decisions. No endless scrolling.

## Who this is for

- Beginners buying their first good bottle
- Casual drinkers who want guidance
- Home bartenders improving their skills
- Collectors who want quick, reliable answers

## Why Spirit Shelf is different

Most platforms are built for experts.  
Spirit Shelf is built for decision making.

You do not need to know tasting terms.  
You do not need to read twenty reviews.  
You get clarity in seconds.
